the design challenge

Alone in a crowd

Create something for university students to combat the paradox of feeling alone in a crowd, and help them interact and connect meaningfully.

30sec

what we found

Our focus: space

Students leave campus about 30 seconds after class. The building gives them no reason to stay, so friendships never get the chance to start.

So we focus on the space itself: turning campus spots into places worth staying in.

Objective & outcome

objective

Get inspiration from students on how the campus can make them feel connected.

outcome

4 ideas for campus spaces that go beyond the usual events and help first-year students start a friendship and actually bond.

how might we

…shape the campus so it lures students into a shared space, where they actually interact and build friendships?

how we customized it

Dressed as a dating show

  • Dating show invitations
  • Printed dead zone photos
  • Speed-dating pairs & timer
  • Museum entrance tickets
  • Dot stickers for voting
  • Red yarn to connect ideas

A playful frame lowers the pressure, so strangers start talking.
